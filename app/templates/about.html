Our Mission

Empowering Earlier, Smarter Heart Health Decisions

Cardiovascular disease is the world's leading cause of death, yet many cases are preventable with early detection. HeartGuard bridges the gap between complex clinical data and actionable health insights making sophisticated risk prediction accessible to healthcare professionals, researchers, and patients alike.

Built on a foundation of rigorous machine learning and validated clinical data, our system analyzes 13 key physiological metrics to generate a personalized risk score in under a second.

What We Offer

Built for Every Stakeholder in Heart Health

From front-line clinicians to research teams, HeartGuard is designed to serve the full spectrum of cardiovascular health needs.

AI Risk Prediction

A trained Random Forest model analyzes 13 clinical features to compute a precise heart disease risk score in real-time.

Multi-Role Dashboard

Separate, purpose-built dashboards for Administrators, Doctors, and Patients — each with the right tools and data access.

Analytical Insights

Deep-dive analytics on patient populations, risk distributions, and prediction trends to support data-driven clinical decisions.

Secure by Design

Role-based access control, encrypted sessions, and strict data separation ensure patient information stays protected.

Progress Tracking

Monitor patient risk trajectories over time with historical records, trend charts, and exportable reports.

Detailed Reports

Generate comprehensive clinical reports with full diagnosis histories, risk breakdowns, and exportable CSV data.
